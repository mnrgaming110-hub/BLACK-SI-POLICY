BLACK SI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

BLACK SI is a tool used to schedule and publish video content to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that its operator is authorized to manage.

Information used

When an authorized operator connects accounts, the tool may process account or Page identifiers, access tokens, publication status, captions, and media URLs required for posting. Videos may be stored with a media hosting provider before publication. The tool is not intended to collect information from the general public or sell personal data.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized publishing requests, upload and publish media, track posting outcomes, and troubleshoot failures. The tool does not use account information for targeted advertising.

Service providers

Publishing requests are processed through Meta's Facebook and Instagram APIs. The operator may use GitHub to run scheduled workflows and Cloudinary to host media files. These providers process data according to their own terms and policies.

Storage and security

Access tokens should be stored as restricted workflow secrets rather than in public code. Posting status and media URLs may be retained in the operator's private project files. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the publishing workflow or applicable obligations. Authorized account owners can request disconnection or deletion of associated information by emailing the contact below. Published content may need to be removed separately from Facebook or Instagram.

Your choices

Account owners can revoke BLACK SI's access using their Meta account or business settings and can request deletion of information held by the operator.

Children

BLACK SI is not designed for children and does not knowingly solicit children's personal information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the workflow or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at this page.
