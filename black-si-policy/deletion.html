BLACK SI — Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: November 19, 2026

To request deletion of information associated with BLACK SI, email mnrgaming11.0@gmail.com with the subject BLACK SI Data Deletion Request. Identify the Facebook Page or Instagram account concerned, but do not send passwords or access tokens.

The operator will review the request, verify account authorization where needed, revoke or remove applicable credentials and locally held account information, and respond with the outcome. Content already published to Meta platforms may require separate deletion there.

You can also revoke access from the relevant Facebook, Instagram, or Meta Business settings.

See the BLACK SI Privacy Policy.
